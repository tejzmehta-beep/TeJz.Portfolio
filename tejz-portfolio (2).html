Side A

The Tracklist

Seven things that get roughly equal airtime in my head — listed like a tracklist, not ranked like a leaderboard.

A1Build

Code

[ Logic & Syntax ]

Coding is the closest thing to a puzzle that fights back. I like the process of breaking a messy problem into small, solvable pieces, and the specific satisfaction of watching broken code finally run. It's taught me patience more than any subject in school has.

"Programs must be written for people to read, and only incidentally for machines to execute."— Harold Abelson
A2Move

Travel

[ New Ground ]

Every new place resets how I see things — different food, different pace, different problems people solve differently. Travel is the subject that never gets boring, because the syllabus changes with every trip.

"The world is a book, and those who do not travel read only one page."— Saint Augustine
A3Learn

Study — Bio Science

[ Class 11, Major: Bio Science ]

Biology answers questions I actually want answered — how living things work, adapt, and survive. Studying it feels less like memorizing a textbook and more like being handed the source code for life itself.

"Study hard what interests you the most, in the most undisciplined, irreverent and original manner possible."— Richard Feynman
A4Play

Cricket

[ Bat, Ball, Backyard ]

Cricket taught me how to lose gracefully and win quietly, long before anything else did. It's a slow-building, strategy-heavy game that rewards patience and reading the moment — skills that carry over into everything else on this page.

"Cricket is not just a game — it's an emotion."— Popular saying
A5Spit

Rap

[ Bars & Wordplay ]

Rap is where the coder and the storyteller in me meet — packing an idea into a tight rhyme scheme is its own kind of debugging. Freestyling especially forces quick thinking, which is a rush of its own.

"Words mean more than what is set down on paper."— Maya Angelou
A6Share

Content Creation

[ Filming, Editing, Posting ]

Turning a random idea into something people actually watch is a satisfying loop — shoot, edit, post, learn, repeat. It's part creative outlet, part crash course in what actually connects with an audience.

"The medium is the message."— Marshall McLuhan
A7Voice

Public Speaking

[ Stage & Mic ]

Getting comfortable in front of a room took time, but it's now one of the things I chase most — the moment nerves turn into momentum. It ties together everything else here: you have to know your subject to speak on it well.

"Courage is what it takes to stand up and speak."— Winston Churchill
